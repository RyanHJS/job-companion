## Technical Manager Review

| Proposal | Verdict | Glaring issues | Proposal-specific questions |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-001 | strong / revise / reject | Technical coherence, depth, support, or missing-context concerns | Questions a technical interviewer is likely to ask |

Reviewers critique the exact proposal independently. They do not silently
rewrite it. Keep unresolved concerns visible when presenting the proposal to
the user. Questions may address new or materially changed proposal wording, but
must not re-verify facts already present in the current resume without a direct
contradiction in another user-provided source. Exclude application-screening and
education-eligibility questions.
