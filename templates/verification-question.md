# Verification Question

Ask one concrete question tied to one requirement:

```yaml
question_id: Q-001
requirement: "requirement being tested"
resume_signal: "why the current resume suggests this may exist"
question: "Did you ...? What did you personally own, for whom, and what happened?"
answer_needed: contribution | scope | deployment | metric | outcome | terminology
```

Prefer questions that recover contribution, scope, outcome, or scale. Avoid
generic prompts such as “Tell me more about this.”
